Privacy

An app about your data should ask for very little of it

Erase.me is built so that you stay in control of every message that leaves your inbox.

Read-only access
We can read your mail to find sign-ups. We cannot send, delete or change anything on our own.
Company names, not email text
We keep which companies you deal with, not what your emails say. Replies to requests Erase.me sent are read only to sort them.
You approve every request
Requests go out from your own Gmail, and only after you tap approve.
Honest about limits
Some companies must keep KYC records by law. We say so before you ask, and we never promise deletion we cannot get.
