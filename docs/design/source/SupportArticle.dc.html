What happens after you send an erasure request

Last updated Nov 16, 2026

When you approve a draft, it is sent from your Gmail to the company’s grievance contact. This is what happens next.

1You approve a draftNothing is sent until you approve it. The request goes from your own Gmail.
2The company has time to replyWe ask for a written reply within 30 days. The tracker shows how many days have passed.
3We sort the replyIt is marked Deleted, Asked for more information, Refused or No reply.
4We prepare a follow-upIf there is no reply by day 30, a follow-up draft is ready for you to approve.
Some companies may have to keep records. Lenders and brokers can be required by law to hold some data. We still send the request, but we cannot promise deletion.
